# Repository Curriculum Audit Report

## 1. Executive Summary
The AI-Native Engineer roadmap is well-structured into 9 progressive phases (00-08). It successfully targets Senior/Staff engineers with a strong systems focus, avoiding basic tutorials. The 4-Tier Lesson Depth Model (High ROI, Important, Advanced, Reference) is clearly established. 

## 2. Structural Analysis
- **Phase 00-01**: Covers foundational hardware, token mechanics, and context engineering.
- **Phase 02**: Covers RAG, Vector Search, and Knowledge Systems.
- **Phase 03**: Covers Tools & MCP.
- **Phase 04**: Covers Agentic Systems and Orchestration (State, WAL).

- **Phase 07**: Covers Production Deployment & LLMOps (Gateways, Edge, Serving).
