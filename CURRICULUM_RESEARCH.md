# Repository Curriculum Research Report

## 1. Research Scope
**Focus**: AI-Augmented SDLC, Agentic Coding Tools (Claude Code, Cursor, Windsurf, Copilot), and the transition from AI-assisted (Software 2.0) to Autonomous Agentic (Software 3.0) workflows in 2026.

## 2. Key Findings & Market Context (2026)
- **The Shift to Agentic SDLC**: Moving beyond simple auto-complete. Agents now handle multi-step planning, refactoring, and PR generation.
- **The Big Three Tools**:
  - **Claude Code**: Terminal-first CLI powerhouse, excellent for complex migrations and multi-file logic.
  - **Cursor**: The leading AI-native IDE with strong multi-file intelligence.
  - **Windsurf**: Agentic IDE with "Cascade" workflow for deep orchestration.
- **The Verification Gap**: With 90%+ adoption, the core engineering challenge is no longer generation, but **verification** (automated quality gates, machine-readable contracts like `AGENT.md`).

## 3. Curriculum Integration Recommendations
- **Phase 08 (AI-Augmented SDLC & Leadership)** must deeply cover the distinction between traditional AI-augmented (human in the loop for every line) vs Agentic SDLC (autonomous multi-file changes with CI/CD gates).
- Emphasize the "Verification Gap" and the use of `AGENT.md` contracts.
- Ensure Phase 08 has clear, non-hyped technical depth for engineering leaders evaluating these tools.

## 4. Verdict
The research supports a strong refactoring of Phase 08 to center around Claude Code, Cursor, Windsurf, and the concept of "Verified Engineering" vs "Vibe Coding".
